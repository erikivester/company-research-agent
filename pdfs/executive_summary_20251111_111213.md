
# Unlocking Value: Reducing Food Waste Across ADM's Global Supply Chain

Archer Daniels Midland (ADM) stands at a critical intersection of global food production and sustainability. With annual revenues exceeding $80 billion and operations spanning over 270 manufacturing facilities worldwide, ADM's influence on the U.S. food system, and its potential to mitigate food waste, is immense. While recent financial reports indicate revenue declines, this presents an opportunity to identify efficiencies and unlock new value streams through targeted food waste reduction strategies. ReFED believes that ADM can leverage its existing infrastructure, commitment to ESG principles, and partnership with the World Food Programme to become a leader in minimizing food waste across its vast supply chain, driving both environmental and economic benefits.

## A Commitment to Sustainability: Building on ADM's ESG Foundation

ADM recognizes the critical link between healthy natural resources and a thriving business, as evidenced by its commitment to sustainable practices and its participation in ESG reporting. While ADM's S&P Global ESG score of 40 (as of August 2025) places it within its industry peer group, there's significant potential to improve this score and demonstrate leadership through concrete food waste reduction initiatives. ADM's annual report highlights a focus on reducing its environmental footprint and redesigning core products with sustainable alternatives. Integrating food waste reduction into these initiatives can further strengthen ADM's ESG profile and attract environmentally conscious investors and consumers.

## Upstream Opportunities: Tackling Waste at the Source

As a major processor of grains and oilseeds, ADM has a unique opportunity to address food waste at the upstream stages of the supply chain – from farm to manufacturing. ReFED's data-driven insights can help ADM identify specific areas where waste occurs during harvesting, processing, and storage. This could include:

*   **Optimizing harvesting practices:** Implementing strategies to minimize crop damage and loss during harvesting.
*   **Improving storage and handling:** Investing in technologies and processes that extend the shelf life of raw materials and reduce spoilage.
*   **Repurposing byproducts:** Exploring opportunities to convert processing byproducts into valuable animal feed, biofuels, or other industrial products.

By focusing on these upstream solutions, ADM can not only reduce waste but also improve the efficiency and profitability of its operations.

## Downstream Impact: Minimizing Waste in Retail and Foodservice

ADM's products are used in a wide range of food and beverage applications, making it a key player in the downstream stages of the supply chain. This provides opportunities to collaborate with retailers and foodservice providers to reduce food waste at the point of consumption. Potential strategies include:

*   **Developing innovative packaging solutions:** Creating packaging that extends shelf life, reduces spoilage, and makes it easier for consumers to use and store products.
*   **Providing consumer education:** Partnering with retailers to educate consumers about proper food storage, meal planning, and reducing food waste at home.
*   **Supporting food donation programs:** Working with food banks and other organizations to donate surplus food to those in need.

## Innovation as a Driver: Leveraging Technology and Circular Economy Approaches

ReFED recognizes the importance of innovation in driving food waste reduction. ADM can leverage its expertise in technology and processing to develop innovative solutions that minimize waste and create new value streams. This could include:

*   **Investing in measurement tools:** Implementing technologies to track and measure food waste at different stages of the supply chain, allowing for targeted interventions.
*   **Exploring circular economy approaches:** Developing closed-loop systems that repurpose food waste into valuable products, such as compost or biogas.
*   **Supporting startups and entrepreneurs:** Investing in or partnering with companies that are developing innovative food waste reduction technologies.

## Partnership Potential: Joining the U.S. Food Waste Pact and Beyond

ADM's existing partnership with the World Food Programme demonstrates its commitment to addressing food insecurity. Expanding this commitment to include food waste reduction can further enhance ADM's social impact. A key step would be joining the U.S. Food Waste Pact, a national initiative led by ReFED and World Wildlife Fund. This would provide ADM with access to a network of leading food businesses, best practices, and resources to help it achieve its food waste reduction goals. Furthermore, ADM can explore partnerships with other organizations, such as food banks and technology providers, to amplify its impact.

## Economic Returns: A Win-Win for Business and the Environment

Reducing food waste is not only environmentally responsible but also economically beneficial. ReFED estimates that for every $1 invested in food waste reduction, businesses can realize a $4 return. By implementing the strategies outlined above, ADM can improve its operational efficiency, reduce costs, and create new revenue streams. This will not only benefit ADM's bottom line but also contribute to a more sustainable and resilient food system.

## Next Steps: A Call to Action

ReFED encourages ADM to take the following steps to accelerate its food waste reduction efforts:

*   **Conduct a comprehensive food waste assessment:** Identify the key sources of food waste across ADM's supply chain.
*   **Set ambitious food waste reduction targets:** Commit to reducing food waste by a specific percentage by a certain date.
*   **Join the U.S. Food Waste Pact:** Gain access to a network of leading food businesses and resources.
*   **Develop a comprehensive food waste reduction strategy:** Outline specific actions that ADM will take to achieve its targets.
*   **Invest in innovation:** Support the development and implementation of new technologies and approaches to reduce food waste.

By taking these steps, ADM can become a leader in food waste reduction and contribute to a more sustainable and equitable food system. ReFED stands ready to partner with ADM on this journey, providing data-driven insights, technical assistance, and access to a network of experts. Together, we can unlock the value of reducing food waste and create a better future for all.
